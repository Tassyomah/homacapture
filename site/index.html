    03 · After you press stop

    Hear the difference.

    The first time a take opens, HomaCapture cleans the voice track. Hum and background noise come out, harsh s sounds soften, and the volume is evened out to the level YouTube and podcasts expect (−16 LUFS, peaks under −1.5 dBTP). The raw track is kept, one click away.

    The clean-up chain, in order

    1. Rumble cut (80 Hz)
    2. Mains hum removed (50/60 Hz)
    3. Noise removal
    4. Softer s sounds
    5. Gentle compressor
    6. −16Even loudness −16 LUFS
    7. Peak limit −1.5 dBTP

      06 · Local first · open source

      It stays on your computer.

      No account, no upload to make it work. A take is a folder on your disk, and every line of the app is public.

      • Storage

        Recordings stay with you

        Every take is saved as plain files in a folder on your computer. Your edits are kept beside it, so the original is never changed.

      • Transcript

        Speech to text runs locally

        Whisper makes the transcript on your computer. Your words are not sent anywhere to be transcribed.

      • Sharing

        Links on your own storage

        Sharing is optional. Connect your own Cloudflare R2 storage once (bucket and keys), and HomaCapture uploads the video and a branded player page there and copies the link for you.

      • Safety

        Crash recovery

        If the app or your computer quits mid-take, the recording is offered back to you the next time you open it.

      • License

        Open source, MIT

        Read it, fork it, build on it. github.com/Tassyomah/homacapture
