What happens after you press stop

You don't edit first and export later. By the time the editor opens, the first pass is already done. Every change can be undone, and the original recording is never touched.

Stop + 0 sautomatic

Zooms follow your clicks

HomaCapture reads every click and cursor move and zooms in where the action is, then eases back out. Stray clicks are ignored.
