The first pass

After youpressstop.

A real kind of take: 31 seconds, a shaky hand, a long pause and an “um”. Scroll, and HomaCapture does the first pass in eight moves. Every change can be undone, and the original recording is never touched.

  1. Screen, mic, camera and computer sound, recorded on one clock. Edge to edge, a shaky cursor, a 4.5 second pause.

  2. Padding, rounded corners and a background behind your screen: indigo, coral, ink or paper.

  3. The shaky path becomes one smooth line, and the cursor is drawn 1.6× larger so people can follow it.

  4. It zooms in on clicks that change something, pans between clicks that sit close together, then eases back out. Stray clicks are ignored.

  5. Hum and noise removed, harsh “s” sounds softened, levelled to −16 LUFS with a −1.5 dBTP limit. The raw track stays selectable.

  6. The Smart Director flags the long pause, the “um” and the “you know”. You accept or reject each one. 0:31 becomes 0:25.

  7. A transcript made on your own computer becomes captions, with the word being spoken highlighted. Off, Minimal or Bold.

  8. Your camera in a circle or a rounded square, in any corner, at any size, mirrored if you like.
