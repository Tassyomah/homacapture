Record once. Let the software make it look good.

HomaCapture records your screen, voice and camera, then polishes the take for you. It zooms where you click, steadies the cursor, cleans up your voice and writes the captions. One recording becomes every version you post.

After you press Stop

Your raw take, finished.

Scroll to watch one recording go from raw to ready. Most of this happens on its own when the recording opens. The rest is one click.

  1. 01

    Raw capture

    Screen, microphone, camera and computer sound are recorded on one clock, so nothing drifts. A floating bar lets you pause and resume. If your computer crashes, the recording is offered back when you reopen.

  2. 02

    Framed

    Padding, rounded corners and a background go around your screen. Your camera sits in a circle or a rounded square, in the corner and size you pick, mirrored if you like.

  3. 03

    Steady cursor

    The cursor is smoothed and drawn larger, and every click leaves a ripple so viewers see what you pressed.

  4. 04

    Zoom that follows

    The view zooms toward the clicks that matter, pans across to clicks nearby, and ignores stray ones.

  5. 05

    Clean voice, captions

    Hum, noise and harsh s sounds come out, and your voice is levelled to −16 LUFS. Whisper writes the transcript on your computer, and the captions light up each word as it is spoken.

  6. 06

    Pauses cut

    Smart Director marks the long silences. Accept, and they leave the timeline. Nothing is deleted, so any cut can be restored.

Voice clean-up

Hear the difference.

The first time a recording opens, HomaCapture cleans the voice track. Hum and background noise come out, harsh s sounds soften, and the level is set to −16 LUFS with peaks held under −1.5 dBTP. The raw track is kept, one click away.

  1. High-pass 80 Hz
  2. Hum notch 50/60 Hz
  3. Noise removal
  4. De-ess
  5. Gentle compressor
  6. −16 LUFS
  7. Limiter −1.5 dBTP

Local first

Your recordings stay with you.

There is no account to make. Recordings save to a folder on your computer and the transcript is made there too. Nothing uploads unless you make a share link.

C:\Users\you\Videos\Narrate\2026-10-20 14.32

mic.clean.wavcleaned, −16 LUFS

events.jsoncursor path, clicks, shortcuts
project.jsonevery edit, as instructions
  • Share links on your own storage. When you want a link, HomaCapture uploads the export to your Cloudflare storage with a branded player page, and copies the link for you.
  • Crash recovery. If the app or your computer stops mid-take, the unfinished recording is offered back when you reopen.
  • Non-destructive. Trims, cuts, zooms and speed changes are saved as instructions. Undo, redo and restore whenever you like.
