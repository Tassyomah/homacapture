      05 · Local first · open source

      It stays on your computer.

      No account, no upload to make it work. A take is a folder on your disk, and every line of the app is public.

      • Storage

        Recordings stay with you

        Every take is saved as plain files in a folder on your computer. Your edits are kept beside it, so the original is never changed.

      • Transcript

        Speech to text runs locally

        Whisper makes the transcript on your computer. Your words are not sent anywhere to be transcribed.

      • Sharing

        Links on your own storage

        When you want a link, HomaCapture uploads the video and a branded player page to your own Cloudflare storage and copies the link for you.

      • Safety

        Crash recovery

        If the app or your computer quits mid-take, the recording is offered back to you the next time you open it.

      • License

        Open source, MIT

        Read it, fork it, build on it. github.com/Tassyomah/homacapture
